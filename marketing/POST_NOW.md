Title: I compared fees on a $20 item (+$0 shipping) on Etsy vs eBay. The flat fees surprised me.

Body:
Pricing a $20 item, the fixed parts changed the answer more than the percentages. From the official pages as of today:
- Etsy: $0.20 listing fee + 6.5% transaction fee (on item + shipping + gift wrap) = $1.50, plus payment processing, which varies by country (check yours).
- eBay (most categories): 13.6% final value fee + $0.40 per order (orders over $10) = $3.12, and that percentage applies to shipping and tax too.
So before payment processing, Etsy takes about $1.50 and eBay about $3.12 on this item. Cheap items get hit hardest by flat fees; above ~$60 the percentages dominate.
Do you include buyer-paid shipping in your fee math? Etsy and eBay both do, which surprised me at first. How do you work out your real take-home?

(Math: Etsy 0.20 + 20*0.065 = 1.50. eBay 20*0.136 + 0.40 = 3.12.)
