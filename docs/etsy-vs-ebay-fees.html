Etsy vs eBay fees on a $20 item

Last checked 2026-10-19. Fee schedules change, so confirm on each marketplace's official page before pricing.

Etsy (US)

  • Listing fee: $0.20 per listing.
  • Transaction fee: 6.5% of the item price plus shipping and gift wrap charged to the buyer.
  • Payment processing: a percentage plus a fixed amount that varies by seller country. We did not confirm the US rate, so it is left out of this example.

Source: Etsy Fee Basics.

Example: 0.20 + 20 × 0.065 = $1.50 before payment processing.

eBay (US, most categories)

  • Final value fee: 13.6% of the total sale amount (item, shipping and sales tax), up to $7,500 per item.
  • Per-order fee: $0.30 on orders of $10 or less, $0.40 above $10.

Source: eBay selling fees. Some categories differ.

Example: 20 × 0.136 + 0.40 = $3.12 (no shipping or tax in this example).

What this shows

Before payment processing, Etsy takes about $1.50 and eBay about $3.12 on this item. Flat fees matter most on cheap items; above roughly $60 the percentages dominate. Try your own numbers in the calculator.
